YOUR PHOTOS GO HERE

THE EASY WAY
1. Copy six photos into this folder.
2. Name them 01.jpg, 02.jpg, 03.jpg, 04.jpg, 05.jpg, 06.jpg.
   That's it — they replace the placeholders by themselves.
   (.jpeg, .png and .webp also work: 01.png, 02.webp, etc.)

THE FLEXIBLE WAY
1. Copy your photos here with any names you like.
2. Open ../config.js and point each "photo:" line at your file, e.g.

     photo: "photos/us-at-the-beach.jpg"

3. Portrait photos (roughly 4:5) look best in the polaroid frames.
4. 01.svg ... 06.svg are pretty placeholders. Leave them — they only show
   when there is no real photo with that number.

Tips:
- Keep file names simple (no spaces or # symbols) to be safe.
- Around 6 photos is perfect; you can add more in config.js.
- Large photos are fine, but 1200px wide is plenty for a phone screen.
